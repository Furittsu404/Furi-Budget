# App icon source

- **Artwork:** `art/icon-source.jpg`, a coin with a plum blossom and a small red plum blossom on its edge, on a dark maroon background.
- **Made with:** Google Gemini image generation, from a text-only prompt (a golden coin with an engraved plum blossom, red-orange glow, dark maroon background). The two flames it first produced were removed, and a small plum blossom added to the coin's corner, with follow-up text prompts. No reference image was uploaded.
- **Date:** 2026-11-27.
- **Reference material:** none. No existing artwork was used as input.
- **Processing:** `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/ic_launcher_art.png` is the source resized to 512 px with the outer edge faded to transparent so it blends into the background colour (`icon_bg`, `#1E0A0B`).
- **Themed and notification icons:** `ic_launcher_mono.xml` and `ic_notification.xml` are simple vector shapes drawn by hand for this app.
